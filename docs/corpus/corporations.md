# Corporations

Night City's real government is a boardroom. Four megacorporations control
the infrastructure that keeps the city alive, and their balance of power
is renegotiated daily through contracts, espionage and the occasional
small war.

## Meridian Systems

Meridian Systems is the city's largest employer. It manufactures the
Mirror line of implants, runs the hospital network, and owns the licensing
body that certifies cyberware surgeons. Meridian's public image is
paternal and clinical; its internal security division is anything but.
Whistleblowers describe the division's retention teams, which persuade
departing engineers to stay through a combination of contract law and
chemistry.

## Vault-X

Vault-X is the data corporation. It operates the tier-1 vaults — the
coldest, most physically secure data storage facilities ever built — and
sells "eternal custody" contracts for corporate archives. A tier-1 vault is
air-gapped, EMP-shielded and buried below the water line, guarded by black
ICE on the inside and private armies on the outside. Vault-X's most famous
product is the Witness Chain, a tamper-evident log of corporate activity
that is admissible in every district court.

## Halberd Dynamics

Halberd Dynamics is the weapons and security corporation. It equips most
district police contracts, manufactures the autonomous Kestrel patrol
drones, and holds the patent on reactive armour used in the Spire's
vehicle convoys. Halberd's CEO famously described the company as "a
firewall with a body count".

## Shin Nexus Logistics

Shin Nexus runs the harbour: cargo cranes, freight tunnels and the
cold-chain that keeps the food markets stocked. It is the smallest of the
four megacorps on paper, but controls of the city's physical supply lines,
which makes it the quiet kingmaker in every district-police negotiation.
Shin Nexus is also the majority shareholder of the desalination plant
through a chain of shell companies.
