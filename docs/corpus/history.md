# A short history of Night City

## Before the Collapse

The city began as a deep-water harbour consortium in the 2040s, a joint
venture between shipping interests and a national government that no
longer exists. The consortium reclaimed land, dug the freight tunnels and
built the first district: Old Town. For a generation the harbour was the
busiest port on the coast.

## The Collapse (2061)

The Collapse was the simultaneous failure of the global financial network
and the public internet in 2061. The proximate cause was never officially
established, though the three most cited theories are a cascade of
fraudulent ledger unwinds, a state-sponsored worm that outgrew its
mission, and simple over-leverage meeting a bad harvest. Whatever the
spark, within ninety days the world's clearing systems stopped settling.

Night City survived because its harbour was already clearing trade in
physical credits rather than ledger money. The consortium's engineers
built the Exchange in the Foundry from salvaged routing hardware, and the
data grid grew outward from it, district by district.

## The Charter Wars (2064–2068)

With no national authority left, the consortium's successor — the
Utilities Commission — attempted to impose a city charter. The
corporations refused. Four years of contract violence followed, called
the Charter Wars by historians and "the paperwork" by everyone who lived
through it. The wars ended not with a treaty but with the Utilities
Accord: the Commission kept the water, the corporations kept everything
else, and the district-police system was born.

## The modern era

The decades since the Accord have been stable by Night City standards.
The megacorporations — Meridian Systems, Vault-X, Halberd Dynamics and
Shin Nexus Logistics — settled into a durable oligopoly. The population
doubled. The grid reached every implant, and the city's unofficial motto,
painted on a Foundry overpass, is: "It kept the light on."
