# Night City

Night City is a coastal megacity of roughly eleven million people, built on
reclaimed harbour land and stacked so densely that street level sees
sunlight only in the financial district. The city is administratively
divided into seven districts, each with its own police contract, power
grid and unwritten laws.

## The districts

The Spire is the corporate core: mirror-glass towers, private security,
and the only reliable power in the city. The Market is the commercial
heart, a canyon of noodle bars, implant clinics and arcade halls that
never closes. The Harbour district is famous for its sushi counters and
street-food markets, and for the smugglers who run them. Kabuto-Row is the
densest residential district, a vertical slum where fixers and gangs
compete for rooftop space. The Foundry is the industrial belt, home to
fabrication plants and the city's unfiltered data-centre cooling exhaust.
Old Town is the pre-expansion historic quarter, under strict (and widely
ignored) preservation codes. The Undergrid is not officially a district:
it is the network of service tunnels beneath the city, inhabited by
squatters, legends and the occasional unlicensed AI.

## Power and policing

Night City has no city-wide government. Each district contracts a private
police force, and the contracts are renegotiated — sometimes with weapons —
every fiscal quarter. The closest thing to a civic authority is the
Utilities Commission, which controls the desalination plant and therefore
the water supply. Its commissioners are elected by anyone holding a
Utilities licence, which is why corporations buy water licences by the
thousand.

## The Undead Hour

Locals call the period between 03:00 and 04:00 the Undead Hour, when the
district power grids rotate their maintenance cycles and the city's neon
goes dark in a slow rolling wave. Superstition says the Undead Hour is when
the data grid dreams; in practice it is simply the cheapest time to move
contraband between districts.
